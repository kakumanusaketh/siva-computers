## 🧭 Navigation Tabs & Core Structure

The website includes dedicated sections corresponding to each menu tab:

1. **Home (`#home`):** Hero spotlight on **All Digital Services**, live stats, and quick links.
2. **About Us (`#about`):** 27+ years legacy, digital services center, and SiCE computer training.
3. **Prints & General Works (`#prints-works`):** High-speed Color Xerox, Photostat, DTP in Telugu & English, Document Translations (Telugu to English & English to Telugu), Spiral Binding, Lamination, Scanning, and Passport size photos.
4. **E-Stamps & Notary Affidavits (`#estamps-affidavits`):** Authorized non-judicial e-Stamps, legal affidavit drafting, rental agreements, private EC & CC retrieval, and online treasury challans.
5. **CSC Works (`#csc-works`):** Authorized **Digi Seva Kendra**, PAN card applications, Aadhaar smart color PVC card prints, Passport online services, DSC tokens, and govt job applications.
6. **Computer Courses (`#courses`):** Authorised SiCE Franchise coaching partner offering 12+ certified courses including **Tally ERP 9.0**, Tally Prime with GST, Python, Java, C/C++, DCA, PGDCA, MS Office, and Spoken English/Hindi.
7. **Bulk Voice Calls, Bulk WhatsApp, Ai Posters (`#bulk-services`):** Automated pre-recorded bulk voice broadcasting, bulk WhatsApp messaging campaigns, AI-generated promotional posters & flyers, and DLT Bulk SMS.
8. **Keyboard Typing (`#keyboard-typing`):** 100% hands-on PC keyboard touch typing training in Telugu & Hindi using **Anu Script with Apple and DOE keyboards only**.
9. **FAQ (`#faq`):** Answers about batch timings, certificates after the successful completion of the course, typing training, and documents needed.
10. **Location (`#location`):** Google Maps embed with exact Piduguralla coordinates and front building facade photo.
11. **Contact (`#contact`):** Interactive inquiry form with direct WhatsApp submission and one-click contact buttons.
